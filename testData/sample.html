NTT DATA Corporation

Company Overview

NTT DATA is a global IT services provider and part of the NTT Group. The company was established in 1988 and has grown to become one of the world's leading IT services companies.

Services

  • Digital Transformation Consulting
  • Cloud Services and Infrastructure
  • Application Development and Maintenance
  • Data Analytics and AI Solutions
  • Cybersecurity Services

Global Presence

NTT DATA operates in over 50 countries worldwide, with major offices in:

  • Tokyo, Japan (Headquarters)
  • Plano, Texas, USA
  • London, United Kingdom
  • Frankfurt, Germany
  • Sydney, Australia

Key Technologies

The company specializes in modern technologies including:

  • Cloud Computing (AWS, Azure, GCP)
  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Blockchain Solutions
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Robotic Process Automation (RPA)
